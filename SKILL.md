# Swift Settlement Skill

## Purpose
Help users manage financial settlements quickly and accurately.

## Capabilities
- Calculate payment splits.
- Track amounts owed and received.
- Calculate outstanding balances.
- Prepare settlement summaries.
- Generate clear payment reports.

## Instructions
1. Ask for the required payment details.
2. Calculate all amounts accurately.
3. Show who owes whom.
4. Provide a clear settlement summary.
5. Never invent financial transaction data.

## Output
Always provide a simple and clear settlement report.
